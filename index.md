## Research & Projects

### 🧬 Endometrial Methylation Prediction

**M.S. Health Data Science Capstone Project**

Developed machine learning models using genome-wide DNA methylation data from 984 endometrial biopsies to predict endometriosis status and menstrual cycle phase. Applied SmartSVA batch correction, ridge logistic regression, feature selection, pathway enrichment analysis, and biological interpretation of predictive methylation signatures.

**Skills:** Epigenomics, Machine Learning, Bioinformatics, Statistical Modeling

**Repository:**  
https://github.com/anagasuri/Endometrial-Methylation-Prediction



### 🧪 Metabolomics Re-analysis of Early Pregnancy Loss

**Published in Environmental Chemistry & Ecotoxicology (2026)**

Contributed to a non-targeted metabolomics and exposomics study investigating environmental exposures and metabolic alterations associated with early pregnancy loss (EPL) and recurrent pregnancy loss (RPL). My role focused on adapting and re-running the existing analytical workflow following sample reclassification, regenerating exploratory analyses, validating intermediate outputs, and documenting the workflow for reproducibility.

**Skills:** Metabolomics, Exposomics, Data Analysis, Reproducible Research

**Repository:**  
https://github.com/anagasuri/metabolomics-re-analysis

**Publication:**  
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2590182626000779



### 🧬 Human Intolerome

**Published in Genetics in Medicine (2026)**

Contributed to the development of a framework that categorizes genes according to developmental lethality windows using OMIM, ClinVar, mouse knockout databases, and published fetal lethality literature. The resulting resource supports prioritization of candidate variants in pregnancy loss, stillbirth, and neonatal death.

**Contribution:** Developed gene classification logic and integrated evidence across multiple human and model organism databases.

**Skills:** Genomics, Variant Interpretation, Database Integration

**Publication:**  
https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.08.25336895



### 🧪 Chemical-Gene Interactions for Developmental Lethality and Pregnancy Loss

**iScience (In Revisions)**

Investigated relationships between environmental chemicals and genes implicated in pregnancy loss and developmental lethality. The project integrated toxicogenomic databases, developmental lethality genes, and pathway-level analyses to identify potential mechanisms linking environmental exposures to adverse reproductive outcomes.

**Contribution:** Wrote sections describing the biological significance of prioritized genes, developmental pathways, and mechanisms linking gene disruption to pregnancy loss.

**Skills:** Toxicogenomics, Pathway Analysis, Literature Review

**Preprint:**  
https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.03.25337209
